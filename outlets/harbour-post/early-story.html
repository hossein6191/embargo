Night ferry to Gull Island returns after three decades

By Mara Quill, transport correspondent

Lantern Bay Ferries is to bring back an evening sailing to Gull Island. From 1 June the company will run a night crossing from the harbour to the island, the first time the route has had a boat after dark in 30 years.

Islanders have asked for a late boat for a long time. The last sailing of the day leaves the harbour in the late afternoon, which means anyone who works on the mainland, or goes to a concert or a hospital appointment there, has to stay over or find a fishing boat willing to take them home.

"It changes what living on the island means," said Tamsin Hale, who runs the island's general store. "My daughter can take a job in town and still sleep in her own bed."

The new crossing will run every night of the week through the summer. The company has not yet said whether it will continue through the winter.
