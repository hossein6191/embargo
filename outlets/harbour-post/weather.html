Weather: sea fog until Thursday, then a bright weekend

By the Harbour Post weather desk

A bank of sea fog will sit over the bay for the first half of the week. Expect thick fog along the quay each morning, lifting a little by midday and rolling back in after tea. Visibility on the coast road will drop below a hundred metres at times, so drive with dipped lights.

The fog clears on Thursday as a dry wind turns round to the north-west. Friday will be cool and bright, with a top temperature of fourteen degrees, and the weekend looks settled: sunshine on Saturday, a few high clouds on Sunday and a light breeze off the land.

Gardeners should hold off planting out tender seedlings for another week. Clear skies on Friday and Saturday night bring a risk of a ground frost in the sheltered valleys inland, though the harbour itself should stay frost free.

The tide tables and the full five-day forecast are on page twelve of the print edition.
