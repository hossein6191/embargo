Lantern Bay Ferries to run a night crossing to Gull Island

By Ivo Brand

Lantern Bay Ferries will add a night crossing to Gull Island from 1 June, the company announced today. It will be the first boat to the island after dark in 30 years.

Gull Island residents have asked for a late boat for years, and the new crossing will run every night through the summer.
